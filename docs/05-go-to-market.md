# LeadGenius — Go-to-Market, Marketing, Sales & Positioning

---

## 1. Brand Positioning

- **Name/wordmark:** LeadGenius. Tagline: **"Outreach that did the homework."**
- **Category framing:** don't enter "AI email writer" (crowded, low-trust). Create/claim **"prospect research automation"** — research is the differentiator, writing is the deliverable.
- **Brand voice = product voice:** specific numbers, hypothesis phrasing, zero hype. If the marketing sounds like the slop our scorer bans, we lose the plot. House rules: no exclamation marks, no "revolutionize/unleash/supercharge", every claim carries a number or a citation.
- **Visual:** dark-first UI aesthetic in all assets; the signature visual is the **live research stream** (findings appearing in real time) — it's our "typing indicator" moment, used in every demo, ad, and post.
- **Enemy narrative:** the villain is *template spam and AI slop*, not competitors. "Every slop email your prospects get makes the well-researched one stand out more — send that one."

## 2. Go-to-Market Plan (first 6 months)

**Strategy: founder-led, service-to-SaaS.** The founder already sells outreach automation (agency + Upwork). LeadGenius is first the *internal tool*, then the *productized service*, then the *self-serve SaaS*. Each stage funds and validates the next.

| Stage | Months | Motion | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dogfood | 0–2 | Run all agency + Upwork client campaigns through LeadGenius | 3 documented case studies with reply-rate numbers |
| Design partners | 2–4 | 10 hand-picked agencies/founders, free Agency tier for feedback + testimonial | Activation > 40%, first 3 payers |
| Public launch | 4 | Product Hunt + LinkedIn case-study storm + lifetime-deal for first 100 | 100 trials, 20 paid |
| Channels | 4–6 | Instantly/Smartlead communities, Clay-adjacent content, affiliate program | Repeatable acquisition, $5k MRR |

**Launch week assets:** 90-second live-research demo video · "How we got 16% replies" teardown post · free slop-checker tool (paste your cold email → score + roast) as viral top-of-funnel.

## 3. Marketing Strategy (channels, ranked)

1. **LinkedIn founder content** (primary — see §5): build-in-public + teardown content to the exact ICP.
2. **Free tools:** the **Cold Email Slop Checker** (paste email → score, banned-phrase highlights, rewrite teaser). Cheap, shareable, feeds signups; SEO magnet for "cold email checker".

4. **Communities:** Instantly/Smartlead Facebook & Slack groups, r/coldemail, cold-email X/Twitter scene. Rule: teach the 16% structure openly; the tool is "how to do this at scale".
5. **Integration marketplaces:** Zapier, Make, HubSpot listings = free durable distribution.
6. **Affiliates:** 20% recurring for cold-email YouTubers/course creators — this niche is influencer-dense and conversion-tracked.
7. **Paid:** only retargeting + branded search until LTV is known. No cold Meta/Google at launch.

## 4. Sales & Client Acquisition Strategy

**The recursive play: sell LeadGenius using LeadGenius.** Every outbound campaign we run is a live demo; the email itself is the proof. Target lists: lead-gen agencies (scrape agency directories), Clay-agency lists, Upwork clients who posted "cold email personalization" jobs.

**Sequence for selling LeadGenius (its own default structure):**
1. Hypothesis opener: "I figured your team writes first lines for ~200 leads a week by hand — mind if I show you what our research engine wrote for 5 of *your* actual prospects?" ← the offer is a **free sample run on their list**: irresistible, zero-risk, product-led.
2. "Wrong person?" bump.
3. Named asset: "personalization audit for {company}".
4. Casual bump.
5. Pivot: time-saved/economics angle.

**Sales-assist motion (Agency & Enterprise):** demo call = live run on the prospect's own leads (never canned data); close with 30-day pilot on Agency tier + onboarding call. Self-serve for Starter/Pro — no calls under $149.

**Activation-as-sales:** the free trial's sample run IS the sales pitch; invest in onboarding like others invest in SDRs.

## 5. LinkedIn Content Strategy (founder account)

**Cadence:** 4 posts/week, 3 content pillars:

1. **Teardowns (2/wk):** real cold emails (anonymized) rewritten live — before/after with the score. Format: screenshot + short caption + one lesson. Highest-performing format in this niche.
2. **Build-in-public (1/wk):** metrics, decisions, roadmap ("Week 6: our slop detector now blocks 41 phrases. Here's the list."). Attracts design partners and talent.
3. **Data/insight posts (1/wk):** from the hypothesis leaderboard — "We analyzed 2,000 cold emails. 'Missed-call revenue' hypotheses got 18% replies. 'AI can help you scale' got 2%." Proprietary data = uncopyable content.

Engagement loop: every post ends with a soft question (practice what we preach); DM opener for engagers: sample-run offer. Repurpose winners into the blog and X.

## 6. Agency Positioning Strategy

Agencies are the wedge market: they feel the pain daily, buy fast, and each one brings N client campaigns.

- **Pitch:** "Your margin is the gap between what clients pay per meeting and what personalization costs you. We collapse that cost from VA-hours to $0.10/lead — and give you a white-label audit report you can sell for $500+."
- **Agency-only assets:** white-label research/audit reports, client sub-workspaces, client-level reporting, "powered by" removal.
- **Partner program:** certified-agency directory (we route inbound leads who want done-for-you to partners) — agencies become evangelists because we feed them clients.
- **Positioning of founder's own agency:** the flagship case study ("we built this because we needed it; here are our campaign numbers"), not a competing service.

## 7. Upwork Positioning Strategy

Upwork is both a revenue channel and a top-of-funnel for SaaS users.

- **Specialized profile** (per the 8-part structure): headline `Cold Email Personalization & AI Lead Research | 16% Reply Rate Systems`. Hook: transformation ("I turn raw lead lists into replies"), proof block with the 45/278 number, qualifier/disqualifier blocks, direct CTA.
- **Productized catalog projects:**
  - "AI-researched personalization for 250 leads — 48h delivery" ($297)
  - "Cold email sequence + research for your niche" ($497)
  - "AI outreach audit of your current campaign" ($197)
  All three are *delivered with LeadGenius* — near-zero marginal labor, 5-star reviews accumulate, and every client is a warm SaaS lead ("want to run this yourself? Here's a code").
- **Proposal strategy:** answer "cold email / personalization / Clay operator" jobs with a **free 5-lead sample run** attached in the first message — the sample is the proposal.
- **Graduation path:** Upwork client → monthly retainer → self-serve Agency subscription.

## 8. Metrics That Govern GTM

| Funnel stage | Metric | Target (month 6) |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | LinkedIn followers / post reach | 5k followers, 50k monthly reach |
| Acquisition | Trials/month | 150 |
| Activation | Signup → first export | > 40% |
| Revenue | MRR / paid accounts | $5k / 35 |
| Retention | Month-2 logo retention | > 80% |
| Referral | % signups from referral/affiliate | > 20% |
| North star | **Approved leads exported per week** | growth 10% w/w |

The north-star metric is chosen deliberately: it captures quality (approved), usage (exported), and value (leads) in one number — vanity-proof.
