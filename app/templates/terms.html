Terms of Service

The short version: use LeadGenius to research businesses and draft honest outreach. Don't use it to spam, and stay within the law wherever your recipients live.

The service

  • LeadGenius researches companies from public sources and drafts outreach sequences for your review. Drafts are suggestions — you review, approve, and send them under your own name and responsibility.
  • LeadGenius does not send email. Exports and integrations hand approved content to your sending tool; sending, deliverability, sender-domain authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), and list-hygiene are yours to manage there.

Acceptable use

  • Upload only business contact data you have a lawful basis to process and contact (GDPR, ePrivacy, CAN-SPAM, CASL and equivalents apply to you as the sender).
  • No consumer lists, purchased personal-data dumps, or data you are not entitled to hold.
  • No use of the research engine to harass, defame, or profile individuals — it exists to understand businesses.
  • No attempts to bypass rate limits, spend caps, or the crawler's robots.txt compliance.

Credits & billing

  • Research consumes credits per lead. Credits are consumed when research runs, including when a site turns out to be thin or unreachable (we still did the work of checking honestly).
  • Paid plans renew until cancelled; cancellation stops future charges and keeps your workspace readable until the period ends.

Honesty of output

  • Research findings link to their sources, and generation refuses to fabricate when evidence is thin — but language models can still err. You are responsible for verifying claims before sending.

Liability

  • The service is provided as-is. To the maximum extent permitted by law, our liability is capped at the fees you paid in the preceding 3 months.
