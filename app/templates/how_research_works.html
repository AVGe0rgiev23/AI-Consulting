How our research works

Every claim in every email traces to a public source you can click. Here is exactly what we do — and what we refuse to do.

Where the information comes from

  • The prospect's own website. We read a handful of public pages — home, about, services, pricing, blog, careers — the same pages any visitor sees.
  • Public web search. Recent news, hiring activity, and reviews from a commercial search API.

That's the entire list. No purchased data brokers, no scraped social profiles, no private or login-gated content.

How the crawler behaves

  • It honors robots.txt, including crawl-delay directives. If a site says no, we don't crawl it — and we tell you that's why research is thin.
  • It identifies itself as LeadGeniusBot with a contact URL, rate-limits itself per domain, and gives up after a short time budget instead of hammering slow sites.
  • Results are cached for 7 days so the same site isn't re-crawled needlessly.

Claim = evidence, enforced in code

  • A human approves everything. The AI drafts; you review, edit, and approve. Nothing is exported or sent without explicit human sign-off on each lead.
  • Search signals carry their publication dates, and stale signals are discounted — a hiring post from last year doesn't masquerade as a fresh buying signal.
  • Every research finding must cite the exact source URL it came from. A validation pass drops any finding whose claim can't be matched to the actual text of that source.
  • Each lead gets a research confidence score (high / medium / low) from pages found, verifiable findings, and search coverage. We show it to you — we don't pretend.
  • When research is too thin to personalize honestly, the app refuses to fabricate. You get a clearly-labeled generic draft flagged for manual review, with the reason (site blocked, domain dead, not enough content).
  • Emails scoring below the quality floor cannot be approved until a human edits them.

Data handling

  • Uploaded lead lists are business contact data processed on your behalf; they stay in your workspace and are never sold or shared.
  • Crawl and search caches expire automatically. Deleting a list deletes its research.
  • Full details in our privacy policy and terms.
